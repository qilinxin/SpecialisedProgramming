In the previous step, all the cats were arranged from left to right. Each cat must move X, so the only options are "original position - X" or "original position + X." To minimize the difference,
I need to find a split point k: at which all cats to the left of k move right, and at k and its right, all cats move left. My solution is to try k from 0 to n. Each time, I linearly scan the new positions
of all cats, recording the minimum and maximum values. The difference between these values is the result. The minimum value among all k is the answer.
